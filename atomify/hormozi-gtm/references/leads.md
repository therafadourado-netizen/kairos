## 1. Core thesis
Advertising = making known. The output that matters is the **engaged lead** (someone who *shows* interest). Two ways to grow: more customers or worth more — this book is about more, better, cheaper, reliable engaged leads. There are only **four ways** one person generates them (Core Four) plus **four Lead Getters** who do it for you. Double your leads, double your business.

## 2. Frameworks

### Lead vs engaged lead
Lead = a person you can contact. **Engaged lead** = shows interest (gives info, follows, replies). Getting more engaged leads is the whole point.

### Offer vs Lead Magnet
- **Core offer** = the main thing you sell. Advertising it directly is the first thing to try.
- **Lead Magnet** = a *complete solution to a narrow problem* that, once solved, reveals a bigger problem your core offer solves. Someone who pays with time now is more likely to pay with money later. Make it so good they feel stupid saying no; give away the secrets, sell the implementation.
- **3 types:** Reveal Their Problem (diagnosis) · Samples & Trials · One Step of a Multi-Step Process.
- **4 delivery methods:** Software · Information · Services · Physical Products (×3 types = up to 12 versions).
- **7 steps:** (0 make it amazing) 1 pick narrow+meaningful problem & who 2 how to solve (pick a type) 3 how to deliver (pick a method, make versions) 4 test the name (headline>image>subheadline) 5 make it easy to consume (every format) 6 make it darn good 7 CTA.
- **CTA** = what to do (clear, direct) + reasons to act now: scarcity (real/ethical), urgency (deadlines/expiring bonuses), and a because-reason.
- Math: advertising a free lead magnet vs the core offer can yield ~10× engaged leads for the same cost (~3× lower CAC).

### The Core Four (Warm/Cold × 1-to-1/1-to-many)
**#1 Warm Outreach (1-to-1, warm)** — 10 steps: get your list (contacts+emails+followers) → pick platform → personalize greeting → **reach out to 100/day** (up to 3×) → warm up with **A-C-A** (Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask) → make an offer (Value Equation) via the **primer**: ask if they *know anybody* who wants [dream outcome] — taking on a few free case studies → make it easy (first 5 free for use+feedback+review) → exhaust one platform → start charging once people refer (80% off next five, raise 20% per five) → keep list warm (9-word email: still looking to [4-word desire]?). Benchmark ≈ 1 customer / 100 reach-outs.

**#2 Post Free Content (1-to-many, warm)** — the *audience* is the compounding asset. **Content Unit = Hook (topic+headline+format), Retain (curiosity via lists/steps/stories), Reward (value per second; nothing is too long, only too boring).** Monetize: give:ask ≥ ~3:1; best = give until they ask (give public, ask private). Ask methods: Integrated (CTA in every piece) / Intermittent (every ~11th). Scale: depth-then-width or width-then-depth. Lessons: "How I" not "How to"; narrow first (puddles→ponds→lakes→oceans); manual > pre-scheduled.

**#3 Cold Outreach (1-to-1, cold)** — the difference is **trust**. 3 steps: build a list (softwares → brokers → elbow grease; first 1,000) → personalize + **Big Fast Value** (blow their minds in <30s; give away stuff people actually pay for) → volume (automate delivery + distribution; follow up more times/ways; re-run list in 3–6 months). Benchmarks: phone ~20% pickup ×25% take = 4/100; email aim ~3% engaged; **min bar = LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1**.

**#4 Paid Ads (1-to-many, cold)** — do last. Make the ad: pick platform → target (lookalike from best customers, then warm/cold; more filters = more efficient but burns faster) → **Callout + Value + CTA** (Callout = attention: labels/yes-questions/if-then/ridiculous results/contrast/likeness; **Value = What-Who-When** [the 8 elements = 4 value drivers + their opposites; show status change via others' eyes; run through past/present/future]; CTA = exact next step + scarcity/urgency/bonuses) → matching **landing page** (continuous experience) → engaged lead. Money: Three Phases — Track → Lose (view it as investing in a money-printing machine; test budget = 2× 30-day cash, kill at 1× if zero leads) → Print (reverse from customer goal, pad ~20%). **LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1**; CAC below 3× industry avg → fix model, above → fix ads. **Client-Financed Acquisition:** recover acquire+fulfill within 30 days (upsell to close the gap → free customers). Don't confuse a sales problem with an advertising problem.

### Core Four on steroids — More · Better · New
Antidote to the Size-of-the-Pie Fallacy. **More = Rule of 100** (100 primary actions/day × 100 days). **Better = fix the constraint** (biggest drop-off), test 1 thing/week/platform, abandon a constraint after 4 tries/1 month. **New** = new placement → platform → activity, only after More & Better. Open to Goal = commit to the outcome regardless of time.

### The Four Lead Getters (leverage)
**#1 Customer Referrals** — lowest cost/highest quality; exponential; grows if referrals > churn. Two reasons you don't get them: product isn't good enough / you don't ask. **Goodwill** = value − price. Six ways to earn referrals (better customers, better expectations, better/faster results, better product, tell them what to buy next). **7 ways to ask:** one-sided / two-sided / ask at purchase / referral as negotiation chip / referral events / ongoing programs / unlockable bonuses.
**#2 Employees** — trade doing for managing → a business that runs without you (an asset). Recruit via the **Internal Core Four**; train via the **3 Ds: Document, Demonstrate, Duplicate.** If they get it wrong, you got it wrong. Key question separating sales vs advertising problem: do my engaged leads have the problem I solve and the money to spend?
**#3 Agencies** — use to **learn** a new method/platform (limited term, pay extra for explanations, train your team, then drop to consulting), not to depend on. Pick checklist: referred with results, waiting list, realistic sales process, long-term strategy, clear tracking, strong offer, expensive.
**#4 Affiliates & Partners** — highest leverage. **6 steps:** find ideal affiliates (who already has my leads?) → make them an offer (a new way to make money) → qualify (make them a customer/expert; certification ≈ 10–20% of an active affiliate's year-1 earnings) → pay from max CAC (**3-tier: 25% / 50% / 100%** on agree / activate / sustain) → launch **Whisper-Tease-Shout** → integrate (give away / sell your lead magnet / sell your core offer, split forever).

## 3. Step-by-step (zero → lead engine)
Grand Slam core offer → decide core vs lead magnet (build magnet if expensive/complex) → start Warm Outreach (100/day, A-C-A, primer, first 5 free, then charge) → add Post Free Content (give until they ask) → add Cold and/or Paid (CFA, ≥3:1) → max via More-Better-New (Rule of 100, test the constraint) → recruit Lead Getters (referrals → employees → agencies → affiliates) → scale; reinvest a fixed % into testing → execute daily via checklist + Open to Goal.

## 4. Decision rules
Rule of 100 · Open to Goal · warm follow-up ≤3× · first 5 free then +20%/five · warm ≈1 customer/100 · cold email aim ~3%, min LTGP:CAC 3:1 · give:ask ≥3:1, give until they ask · post ≥1×/day · re-run cold lists 3–6 mo · LTGP:CAC ≥3:1 scale threshold · CAC vs 3× industry avg diagnostic · CFA within 30 days · paid test budget 2× 30-day cash (kill at 1×) · 1 test/week/platform, abandon after 4 tries · affiliate cert 10–20% · affiliate payout 25/50/100% of max CAC · referrals > churn (aim ≥25% from referrals) · More & Better before New · paid ads last · expect 3–6 mo to crack a new source · stick with a pick for years.

## 5. Review checklist (lead-engine audit)
Leads vs engaged leads measured? Grand Slam core offer? Strong lead magnet (narrow problem, multi-format, tested name, CTA)? All Core Four running or one maxed — which at zero? Warm ~100/day A-C-A+primer, list kept warm? Content ≥daily, high give:ask? Cold: targeted list + Big Fast Value + volume + multi-channel follow-up + 3–6 mo re-runs? Paid: right platform/targeting, Callout-Value-CTA, matching landing page, tracking? LTGP:CAC per channel ≥3:1? CFA within 30 days + upsell? Rule of 100 / Open to Goal? Testing 1 thing/week on the constraint with a log? More & Better exhausted before New? Referrals: product good enough, you ask (7 ways), referrals>churn, ≥25%? Employees: 3 Ds + Internal Core Four + cost/lead tracked? Agencies: used to learn with a deadline + team-training? Affiliates: ideal list, money-making offer, qualification, tiered payout, launch, integration? Is low volume/effort the real reason something "doesn't work"?

## 6. Common mistakes
Not advertising enough · confusing leads vs engaged leads · weak lead magnet / scared to give secrets · advertising only the core offer · never doing warm outreach · dismissing content (the audience is the asset) or asking for money too soon · pre-scheduling / blaming short attention spans · cold: underestimating volume+time, weak lead magnet, contacting once · paid: chasing creative perfection over efficiency, quitting winners early / running losers long, confusing sales vs advertising problem · mistaking bad model (low LTGP) for bad ads (high CAC) · Size-of-the-Pie Fallacy · testing many things at once / improving non-constraints · employees: if-you-want-it-done-right thinking · referrals: overrating product, never asking, neglecting the back end · agencies: dependency trap, being cheap · affiliates: not getting them invested, no launch, capping payouts · quitting/switching after a few losses.
